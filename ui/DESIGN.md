## Brand & Style

This design system serves multi-generational Indian enterprises, distributors, and modern retail merchants transitioning into high-velocity digital commerce. The tone balances deep commercial credibility with the distilled craft of modern editorial software. It avoids the visual noise of generic enterprise dashboards, opting instead for quiet confidence, tactile surfaces, and warm hospitality.

The visual approach merges **Minimalism** with **Tactile Modernism**:
- **Generous Space & Deliberate Density:** Interfaces feel calm yet informative, presenting operational clarity without intimidating complexity.
- **Editorial Poise:** Typographic scale and pacing recall high-end trade publications (Monocle, Kinfolk) anchored by the exacting precision of modern software platforms (Stripe, Linear).
- **Warmth Over Clinical White:** Replaces cold, sterile grays with sunlit bone, unbleached linen, and earthen terracotta tones that evoke permanence, dignity, and established generational success.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an earthy, warm architectural environment:
- **Canvas & Backdrops:** Base application canvas utilizes layered warm ivory (`#FBF9F5`, alternating into `#F7F6F2` and `#F5F4EF` for contextual depth). Subtle sunlit ambient halos use low-opacity radial gradients of warm peach (`#FED7AA` at 15–25% alpha).
- **Ink & Typography:** High-contrast charcoal (`#18181B`) for primary headings and critical transactional copy, paired with muted stone gray (`#52525B` and `#71717A`) for secondary metadata, descriptions, and supporting tables.
- **Brand Accents:** Warm terracotta/burnt orange (`#EA580C` with a softer `#E07A5F` variant) signals primary momentum, call-to-action focus, and active status. Muted emerald (`#16A34A`) provides fiscal assurance for margins, surplus, and positive reconciliations.
- **Cards & Inverted Fields:** High-grade white (`#FFFFFF`) cards resting on fine hairline boundaries (`#E6E4DD`). Anchoring hero cards and bottom conversion bands leverage dark charcoal surfaces (`#121214`) accented by faint amber ambient glows.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** throughout, delivering geometric purity softened by humanist touches. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (-0.02em to -0.035em) produce a commanding editorial presence reminiscent of leading financial broadsheets and refined technology journals.
- **Body Rhythm:** Generous line heights (1.5x to 1.55x) ensure sustained reading comfort for operational overviews, ledger explanations, and merchant case studies.
- **Eyebrows & Overlines:** Rendered in compact all-caps (`label-eyebrow`) with +0.08em tracking to introduce clear categorical divisions before large headline anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with a maximum content container of 1240px:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, minimum 32px (`2rem`) page margin. Section padding relies on deliberate, uncrowded vertical pacing (`4.5rem` to `7rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px margin. Multicolumn feature blocks collapse gracefully into 2-column or 4-column balanced sets.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) canvas margins. Side-by-side metric tiles collapse into vertically stacked cards with touch-target safeguards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through precise surface separation, low-contrast structural boundaries, and warm ambient light rather than artificial drop shadows:

- **Hairline Framing:** Primary cards and elevated regions use crisp `1px` borders in warm stone (`#E6E4DD`). Dark surfaces use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Restrained Ambient Shadow:** When surfaces elevate on hover or sit in overlay mode, they utilize dual-tier diffused shadows tinted with raw umber:
  - Base Card: `0 1px 3px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 35, 25, 0.04)`
  - Elevated Dropdown / Modal: `0 4px 12px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06), 0 20px 36px -8px rgba(35, 25, 15, 0.08)`
- **Warm Glows (Atmospheric Depth):** Dark conversion segments employ an interior, ultra-diffused radial halo (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.12), transparent 70%)`) to create tactile warmth without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometry:
- **Interactive Controls:** Action buttons, pill-chips, and search inputs feature fully rounded caps (`9999px`) to create an inviting, tactile touchpoint.
- **Cards & Modules:** Medium-to-large containers implement an intentional `1rem` to `1.5rem` (16px–24px) border radius, softening high-density commercial data into digestible modules.
- **Data Tables & Nesting:** Inner input cells and sub-containers inherit an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius to preserve crisp structural alignment inside parent cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Dark tactile pill (`#18181B`) with ivory text (`#FBF9F5`), subtle inset highlight on top (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`), and 0.5rem x 1.25rem padding. Transitions on hover to subtle charcoal lift.
- **Terracotta Accent:** For key transactional conversions, uses rich burnt orange (`#EA580C`) text on pure white, or solid terracotta with white typography.
- **Secondary Outline:** Transparent background, 1px warm border (`#E6E4DD`), charcoal text, with background transition to warm ivory (`#F7F6F2`) on hover.

### Cards & Metrics
- **Metric Cards:** Crisp white background, `1px solid #E6E4DD`, 20px radius. Header houses an uppercase label and an emerald trend indicator; the metric value is displayed in bold 32px charcoal.
- **Editorial Quote Modules:** Warm tinted background (`#F7F6F2`), 24px radius, without hard borders. Large quotation typography followed by merchant portrait, signature block, and location marker.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding. Subtle muted emerald green (`rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.1)`) with `#16A34A` text for healthy metrics; warm peach/terracotta tint for active notifications.

### Accordions & Dividers
- Hairline dividers (`1px solid #E6E4DD`) running edge-to-edge. Expansion triggers feature smooth chevron rotations with no background fills, maintaining editorial white space.

### Form Inputs
- 48px height, rounded pill or 10px soft corners. Pure white surface with `#E6E4DD` boundary, transitioning to a focused terracotta outline ring (`#EA580C` at 2px with soft 4px blur) upon interaction.
